# AurumDesk AI - Company Overview

AurumDesk AI is a fictional financial technology company that builds AI-powered tools for trading teams, portfolio managers, and market analysts.

The company focuses on helping human decision-makers understand market context faster. AurumDesk AI does not execute trades automatically and does not provide guaranteed trading signals.

## Mission

AurumDesk AI helps financial teams reduce research time, organize market information, and make more disciplined decisions.

## Core Products

1. AI Market Brief
   A daily market summary tool that analyzes news, macroeconomic events, market sentiment, and major asset movements.

2. Risk Desk Assistant
   An internal assistant that helps trading teams understand risk rules, drawdown limits, and account restrictions.

3. Support Intelligence Layer
   A knowledge assistant for customer support teams that answers questions based on approved company documents.

## Important Company Principles

- Human traders remain responsible for final decisions.
- AI output must be explainable and traceable to company-approved documents.
- The system should avoid hallucination when company documentation does not contain the answer.
- Risk management is more important than short-term profit.
- Client trust depends on clear communication and transparent limitations.

## What AurumDesk AI Does Not Do

AurumDesk AI does not promise profits, does not manage client funds directly, and does not provide personalized financial advice.
