# Client FAQ

## Does AurumDesk AI give trading signals?

No. AurumDesk AI does not provide guaranteed trading signals. It provides research assistance, market summaries, and risk context.

## Can AurumDesk AI place trades automatically?

No. AurumDesk AI does not execute trades automatically. Human users are responsible for final trading decisions.

## Is the AI Market Brief financial advice?

No. AI Market Brief is not personalized financial advice. It is a research and information tool.

## What markets does AurumDesk AI cover?

The fictional product can summarize information about equities, forex, commodities, crypto, and macroeconomic events.

## What happens if information is missing from the company docs?

If information is missing from the approved company documents, the knowledge bot should say that it could not find the answer in the company knowledge base.

## Why does the bot show sources?

The bot shows sources so users can understand which company document supported the answer. This improves trust and reduces hallucination risk.
