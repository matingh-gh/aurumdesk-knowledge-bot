# Product Guide - AI Market Brief

AI Market Brief is a fictional product by AurumDesk AI that creates structured market summaries for traders and analysts.

## Purpose

The product helps users understand important market developments quickly. It summarizes relevant news, macro events, asset movements, and sentiment changes.

## Main Features

1. Daily Market Summary
   Provides a concise summary of major developments in equities, forex, commodities, crypto, and macroeconomic events.

2. Asset Watchlist Context
   Allows users to focus on selected assets such as EUR/USD, NASDAQ, S&P 500, gold, oil, or Bitcoin.

3. Risk Highlight
   Flags upcoming high-impact events that may increase volatility.

4. Explanation Layer
   Explains why a market event may matter, instead of only listing headlines.

5. Source-Aware Responses
   Answers should be based on company-approved documents or connected research sources.

## Limitations

AI Market Brief does not guarantee prediction accuracy.

It should not be used as the only basis for entering or exiting trades.

The product does not execute trades and does not replace human judgment.

## Ideal User

The ideal user is a trader, analyst, or portfolio team member who already understands basic financial markets but wants faster research workflows.
